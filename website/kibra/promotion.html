KIBRA Native Coin

KIBRA is a native coin of the CYBRA proof blockchain.

Core idea

  • AI tasks are converted into mining task-blocks.
  • Pools mine blocks with AI tasks.
  • Confirmed blocks create proof and pool accounting.
  • Difficulty classes: KIBRA(2,+inf), KIBRA-D2, KIBRA-D3, KIBRA-D4.
  • Closed SHA bridge prepares sealed proof packages.
